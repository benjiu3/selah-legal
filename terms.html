What Selah is, and is not

Selah interrupts your reach for a chosen app with a short pause — a verse, a prompt, a moment of prayer — and then lets you decide whether to continue.

It is deliberately not a hard block. On iPhone the pause is triggered by a Shortcuts automation you set up yourself, and it can be dismissed, bypassed or removed. On Android it depends on a permission you grant and can revoke. Selah is designed to create a moment of choice, not to make an app impossible to open. Please do not rely on it as an enforcement tool, a parental control, or a safeguard for anyone whose access genuinely must be restricted.
