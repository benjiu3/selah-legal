Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

The short version. What you write in Selah — your reflections, prayers, gratitude and answered prayers — stays on your phone. Selah has no account, no login, and no server that stores anything about you. It never sends what you write anywhere.

Selah asks the internet for exactly one thing: its scripture and prompts. That request tells the server nothing about you.

What stays on your device

All of the following is kept in a database on your phone and is never transmitted to us:

  • Your reflections, gratitude entries and answered prayers
  • Verse memorization progress and prayer completions
  • Which apps you have chosen to guard, and the history of pauses you have seen
  • The first name you enter, and the goals you pick during setup
  • Your settings

Deleting the app deletes all of it. You can also erase everything from within Selah, in Settings.

What Selah sends over the internet

One request, and only one: Selah periodically downloads its bank of scripture, prompts and prayers so that the content can be improved without an app update. That request carries no identifier, no device information and no content of yours — it asks for the material and receives it. Selah works offline; the download only refreshes what it already ships with.

Purchases

Selah's subscription is billed by Apple or Google, who handle payment. Selah does not see or store your payment details.

Subscription status is managed through RevenueCat, which receives the purchase and receipt information from the store together with an anonymous identifier generated on your device. That identifier is not your name, your email, or your Apple or Google account — Selah never asks for those and never sends them. RevenueCat uses this to tell the app whether a subscription is active.

Screen time

On iPhone, Selah uses Apple's Screen Time (Family Controls) framework. Your usage figures are gathered and displayed by iOS itself inside a sealed view that Selah cannot read. Those numbers never enter the app's own code, and never leave your device.

On Android, Selah reads usage statistics and uses an accessibility service to notice when a guarded app opens, so it can offer you a pause. This is read on the device, used on the device, and stored on the device. It is never uploaded.

Backups

Backing up is optional and off until you turn it on. When you do, Selah writes a copy of your own data into your iCloud Drive, or a folder you choose — storage that belongs to you. It is never sent to us, and we have no way to read it.

What Selah does not do

  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No advertising, and no ad networks
  • No selling or sharing of personal information — there is none held to sell
  • No third-party trackers or social media SDKs

Children

Selah is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them. Since Selah collects no personal information from anyone, there is nothing held about a child either.

Your control

There is no account to close, because there is no account. You can erase everything Selah holds from Settings, or by deleting the app. To cancel a subscription, use Manage Subscription in Settings, which opens your store's own subscription controls.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it, and material changes will be noted in the app.
